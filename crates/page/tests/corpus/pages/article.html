Why small browsers matter

By A. Writer, 21 October

A browser is mostly a reading machine. Most of what people open is text: news, documentation, recipes and long essays. If a small engine gets that right, it is already useful.

What it takes

Reading needs a parser that forgives broken markup, a cascade that applies styles in the right order, and flow layout that wraps lines where people expect.

  • Parse the page as it streams in.
  • Style it with the cascade.
  • Lay out blocks and lines, then paint.

Simple things should be simple.

Layout

Blocks stack down the page; their vertical margins collapse. Inside a block, text and inline boxes fill line boxes from left to right.
